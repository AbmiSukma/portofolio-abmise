Waddle: Gamified Study Companion App

Waddle is a gamified mobile study companion concept aimed at Indonesian students who lose study consistency: motivation decays when progress is invisible and planning feels like homework. The design centers a friendly penguin mascot and Bahasa Indonesia microcopy ('Semangat Belajar!') to make the product feel like a study buddy, not a tracker.

Core Loop & Features

The core loop of the application is designed to maintain user engagement through achievable milestones and clear feedback:

  • Pomodoro Focus Session: Time-boxed learning blocks (e.g. 'Sesi 3 dari 4').
  • Flashcards for Active Recall: Contextual memorization aids (e.g. 'Mitokondria').
  • Lesson-Completion Moments: Celebration feedback to create a sense of achievement.
  • Daily-Plan Goal Board: Equipped with a calendar strip for a clear view of study habits.
  • Learning Score Screen: Pairs progress bars with warning copy when a habit is breaking, so the app notices decay before the user does.

Visual Language & Craft

The visual language utilizes soft pastel blues, rounded card surfaces, and a dashed journey curve tying sessions into one narrative. The craft focus was motion and state: every screen answers ‘what just happened, what next?’ with an explicit celebration or nudge, turning solo studying into a visible, streak-driven journey.
